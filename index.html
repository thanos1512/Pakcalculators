Calculators built on Pakistani rules

Most calculators online are built for other countries. These are built around the rules that apply here: FBR's salary tax slabs, NEPRA's electricity tariff, FPSC and PPSC age relaxation, and board and university grading. Each page lists the date its figures were last checked and links to the sources.

Which calculator do I need?

  • Checking a payslip or a job offer: the salary tax calculator shows the tax deducted each month under the 2026-27 slabs and your take-home pay.
  • Applying for a government job: the govt job age calculator works out your age on the closing date and adds FPSC or PPSC age relaxation.
  • Planning this month's bijli bill: the electricity bill calculator estimates the bill from your units and warns you when a few units fewer would move you to a cheaper slab.
  • Paying zakat: the zakat calculator adds up cash, gold, silver and business stock against the nisab.
  • Result day: the marks percentage calculator gives your percentage and grade, and the GPA and CGPA calculator does the same for university semesters.
  • Filling in a form: the age calculator gives your exact age in years, months and days on any date.

Are the results accurate?

Every calculator is tested against worked examples, including examples published by the official bodies themselves. The results are still estimates: your employer, your electricity company or the recruiting commission has the final figure. Read how the figures are checked.
